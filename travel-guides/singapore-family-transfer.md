# KelanaAI Singapore Family Airport Transfer — Demo Policy 2026

This internal demo guide describes the `KFS-24` family transfer product.

## Arrival service

- Meeting point: Changi Airport Terminal 3, Basement 1, Door 6.
- The driver carries a navy KelanaAI sign with the lead passenger's first name.
- Booking cutoff: 18:00 Singapore Time on the calendar day before arrival.
- Complimentary waiting time: 75 minutes after the flight's actual landing time.
- One folded stroller and one child safety seat are included at no extra charge.
- Maximum luggage: four standard suitcases plus two cabin bags for each vehicle.
- Oversized sports equipment must be declared at least 24 hours before arrival.

## Delay procedure

Flight delays are monitored automatically. If immigration or baggage collection will take longer than 75 minutes, send the booking code to the KelanaAI operations chat before the waiting period ends.
