# Nusantara Shield Travel Insurance — Demo Policy NS-2026

This is a fictional policy created for the KelanaAI RAG demonstration. It is not an insurance contract.

## Benefits

- Trip delay benefit starts after a delay of 6 consecutive hours.
- Eligible meals and local transport are reimbursed up to USD 180 per insured traveler.
- Baggage loss is covered up to USD 600, with a USD 150 limit per individual item.
- Emergency dental treatment caused by an accident is covered up to USD 350.
- Sports equipment rental is covered up to USD 90 when checked sports equipment is delayed for more than 12 hours.

## Claim requirements

A claim must be submitted within 14 calendar days after returning home. Include the booking confirmation, carrier delay or loss report, original receipts, and the completed claim form. Purchases without an itemized receipt are not reimbursable.
