# KelanaAI South Korea Culture Pass — Demo Policy 2026

This internal demo guide is valid for KelanaAI training exercises only.

## KSC-72 pass

- Product code: `KSC-72`.
- Price: KRW 89,000 per adult and KRW 54,000 per child aged 6–12.
- Validity: 72 consecutive hours, starting from the first attraction scan.
- Physical pass pickup: Hongik University Station, Exit 3, at the orange KelanaAI counter.
- Pickup hours: 08:30–18:00 Korea Standard Time.
- Included attractions: Gyeongbokgung Palace, National Museum of Korea, N Seoul Tower observatory, Bukchon guided walk, and a Han River evening cruise.
- The Han River cruise must be reserved at least 24 hours before departure.
- Refunds are allowed until 48 hours before the selected pickup date; after that the pass is non-refundable.

## Required pickup documents

Show the booking QR code and the passport used for the reservation. A photo of the passport is not accepted.
