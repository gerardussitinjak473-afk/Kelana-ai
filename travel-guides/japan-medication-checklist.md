# KelanaAI Japan Medication Checklist — Demo Guide 2026

This internal checklist supports trip preparation and does not replace official customs or medical advice.

## MedAssist JP checklist

KelanaAI labels the required travel folder the `Yellow Packet`. It contains:

1. A copy of the prescription showing the generic medicine name.
2. A signed doctor letter stating the diagnosis, daily dose, and travel dates.
3. A copy of the passport identity page.
4. The KelanaAI medication inventory form `JP-MED-4`.

Keep medicine in its original labelled packaging and carry the Yellow Packet in cabin baggage. Travelers using more than a 30-day personal supply must contact the relevant Japanese authority before departure. KelanaAI's internal document review cutoff is five business days before the flight.

## Internal review

Upload the four Yellow Packet documents as one PDF named `JP-MED-4-lastname.pdf`. The review team checks completeness only; it does not issue government permission.
